## 1. Composition rule

Select one value from each applicable axis:

`surface × business category × business model × decision mode × conversion × platform × art direction`

Example:

`storefront × fashion × focused DTC × fit/style-led × direct-commerce × Shopify × editorial-retail`

This creates a reusable decision system. Do not create a dedicated skill for `fashion Shopify`, `keyboard 3D`, `pet marketplace`, or another individual combination.

## 5. Decision mode

Choose the dominant buyer/user decision. It controls hierarchy and proof.

| Decision mode | Put early | Strong proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specification/compatibility | specs, comparison, supported systems, variants | measurements, standards, compatibility data, demos |
| Size/fit | sizing, dimensions, fit guidance, returns | measurements, fit notes, real product imagery |
| Ingredient/safety | ingredients, allergens, claims, usage, warnings | certification/source, lab or regulatory evidence, transparent policy |
| Taste/style | imagery, curation, variants, context | real photography, editorial selection, customer evidence |
| Provenance/material | origin, maker, process, material, longevity | traceable source, craft/process documentation |
| Price/promotion | price, savings basis, availability, delivery | honest comparison, terms, stock and fulfilment facts |
| Locality/availability | location, coverage, hours, schedule | address/service area, availability, operational credentials |
| Authority/trust | expertise, method, governance, risk | credentials, attributable case work, policies, named experts |
| Outcome/performance | problem, mechanism, result, adoption path | sourced results, demonstration, case context |
| Discovery/curation | search, taxonomy, categories, recency, saves | breadth, quality signals, transparent ranking |
| Community/identity | people, participation, norms, belonging | real members, activity, moderation and safety rules |
| Occasion/emotion | date, story, atmosphere, package, logistics | real venue/work, schedule, inclusions, social evidence |

Use at most two decision modes. One owns the first screen and mobile priority.

## 6. Conversion

Select from [`conversion-funnels.md`](conversion-funnels.md): lead generation, click-through, subscription, video-led, direct commerce, or discovery. Booking, donation, ticketing, and account creation may replace the final action while following the same commitment/proof principle.

## 7. Implementation platform

Treat platform as an adapter, not an art direction or industry.

- WordPress/WooCommerce: preserve theme hooks, templates, blocks, checkout/plugin boundaries.
- Shopify: preserve theme sections, Liquid data, product/variant/cart semantics, checkout boundaries.
- BigCommerce: preserve catalogue/channel and checkout integration boundaries.
- Adobe Commerce/Magento: expect complex catalogue, price, customer-group, and extension constraints.
- Drupal Commerce/Ubercart: preserve entity/view/configuration conventions.
- Easy Digital Downloads: prioritise licence, file delivery, version/update, and account states.
- Ecwid/embedded commerce: preserve host-site and embedded-store boundaries.
- OpenCart, PrestaShop, VirtueMart, Zen Cart: inspect the existing theme/module architecture before editing.
- Jamstack/headless: identify the commerce/CMS backend, caching, preview, webhook, search, and checkout ownership.
- HTML/static: do not imply working inventory, account, checkout, CMS, or form infrastructure unless implemented.
- Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, Photoshop: treat as source artifacts. Extract tokens/components/constraints; they are not runtime platforms.

Never choose a platform from appearance. Detect it from the project or use the platform the user names.

## 8. Art direction

Choose the genre/theme normally. When the user requests expressive, trend-led, colourful, immersive, or 3D work, load [`style-directions.md`](style-directions.md). Art direction never replaces category, decision, proof, or platform rules.

## 9. Category profiles

Use these compact profiles when no specialist domain pack exists:

| Category | Typical primary job | Content/proof emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate/B2B | understand capability and establish trust | capabilities, industries, governance, case evidence, contact path |
| Technology/SaaS | evaluate outcome and adoption | product evidence, workflow, integrations, security, pricing/adoption |
| Entertainment/media | discover and consume/watch/listen/attend | catalogue/programme, talent, schedule, release/ticket facts |
| Nonprofit/civic | understand cause and participate | mission, current work, impact evidence, funding transparency, donate/volunteer |
| Education/course | assess learning fit and enrol | outcomes, curriculum, instructor, schedule, prerequisites, credential |
| Food/hospitality | choose venue/menu/stay and reserve | menu/rooms, location, hours, availability, dietary/accessibility facts |
| Travel/booking | compare itinerary, availability, and conditions | dates, route, inclusions, policy, price, local proof |
| Real estate/property | discover and qualify a property | location, price, dimensions, availability, plans, agent/developer trust |
| Healthcare/wellness | assess suitability, safety, and access | services, clinician credentials, eligibility, risks, location, booking |
| Finance/insurance | understand value, eligibility, cost, and risk | fees/rates, terms, security, regulation, calculators, disclosures |
| Community/directory/jobs | discover entities and participate/apply | search/filter, profile quality, activity, safety, application/contact state |
| Event/wedding | understand atmosphere, logistics, and availability | date, venue, programme/packages, work examples, inclusions, enquiry/ticketing |
| Automotive/mobility | compare compatibility, condition, price, and access | specifications, fitment, inventory, financing/rental terms, inspection |
| Industrial/logistics | qualify capability and operational fit | capacity, standards, process, geography, lead time, quote path |

## 10. Output contract

Before choosing macrostructure, emit one composition line:

`Surface: <x> · category: <x> · model: <x> · decision: <x> · platform: <x or unknown> · conversion: <x> · proof: <x> · direction: <x>`

If an axis is unknown, say `unknown` or infer it transparently. Do not hide uncertainty by selecting a familiar template.
