# Conversion funnels

Choose one primary conversion mode after the domain. This file controls hero action, proof order, form depth, and success state; it does not choose the visual theme.

## Lead generation

Use when the business must qualify a prospect before pricing or delivery.

- Hero: specific outcome, audience, short qualification form or a CTA to it.
- Ask only what changes routing: name, contact channel, one scope selector, and one context field at most.
- Show privacy/response expectations beside the form.
- Success: confirm receipt, response window, and next step; never end at a generic toast.
- Suitable domains: consulting, agencies, local services, high-consideration B2B.

## Click-through

Use when the landing page should route the visitor to a higher-intent destination.

- Hero CTA names the destination: `View services`, `Check availability`, `Compare plans`, or `Book a call`.
- Repeat the same destination after the strongest proof section, not after every section.
- Preserve query/campaign context when routing.
- Do not disguise a navigation action as form submission.

## Subscription

Use for newsletters, reports, waitlists, replenishment, or recurring access.

- Explain frequency, content/value, price if any, and cancellation terms before submission.
- Default to one email field; request profile data after confirmation.
- Provide loading, validation, duplicate-email, success, and retry states.
- Never use a fake scarcity timer or pre-checked marketing consent.

## Video-led

Use when motion demonstrates the product, method, transformation, or credibility better than prose.

- Put a real poster frame, duration, captions/transcript, and a plain-language title on the video.
- Treat video as evidence, not ambient decoration.
- Never autoplay with sound. On mobile, prefer a poster and user-initiated playback.
- Place the CTA beside or immediately after the evidence the video supplies.

## Direct commerce

Use when the visitor can purchase without sales contact.

- Hero or collection entry leads to products, not a generic brand form.
- Preserve price, variant, stock, shipping, return, and delivery facts near the purchase action.
- Cart and checkout actions outrank newsletter capture.
- Success: visible cart update with undo/removal path; do not interrupt with celebratory modal noise.

## Discovery

Use when browsing is the product: marketplaces, large catalogues, directories, editorial collections, or portfolios.

- Search, categories, filters, featured/recency surfaces, and saved state are primary.
- Maintain filters and scroll position when returning from detail pages.
- Prefer progressive disclosure over a single overloaded mega-grid.
- Make empty, no-result, loading, and partial-data states explicit.

## Conflict rule

If two goals compete, identify one primary and one secondary. The primary owns the hero and persistent mobile action. The secondary may appear once after sufficient proof. Never give two equal-weight primary CTAs.
