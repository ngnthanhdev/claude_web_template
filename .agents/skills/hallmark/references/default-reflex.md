# Default-reflex interruption

Run this gate after domain/funnel selection and before macrostructure selection. Its purpose is not novelty; it prevents familiar AI patterns from becoming decisions without evidence.

## The question

Ask internally:

> Am I repeating an AI habit, or does this choice follow from the audience, domain, content, conversion goal, and available proof?

## Five-step gate

1. **Name the attractor.** State the first familiar layout, colour, component, copy, or motion pattern that came to mind.
2. **Find its evidence.** Identify which supplied fact or user need requires it. Familiarity is not evidence.
3. **Test a categorical alternative.** Consider at least one structurally different solution, not a colour swap.
4. **Keep or reject.** Keep the attractor only when it is the clearest answer and explain why. Otherwise choose the evidence-backed alternative.
5. **Record one line.** Add the result to the preview under `Default reflex`.

## Common attractors to challenge

- Centred hero with eyebrow, headline, subhead, two CTAs, and dashboard mockup.
- Purple/blue gradient, aurora mesh, glass cards, or floating 3D orbs used to signal innovation.
- Three equal feature cards with generic icons.
- Pill-shaped navigation and buttons everywhere.
- Bento grid selected before content has natural differences in size or priority.
- Logo wall, metrics, testimonials, awards, or review stars without supplied proof.
- Identical section padding and repeated card treatment down the whole page.
- Fade-up-on-scroll on every section and `hover:scale` on every card.
- Four-column SaaS footer on a business with no real sitemap.
- A carousel used to hide excessive content instead of editing or structuring it.

## Domain-aware counter-moves

- **Commerce:** let taxonomy, comparison, product imagery, specifications, availability, and buying state determine the layout.
- **Local services:** let coverage, availability, process, and contact urgency determine the layout.
- **Professional services:** let method, expertise, case evidence, and commitment level determine the layout.
- **Creative work:** let actual projects, authorship, and point of view determine the layout.
- **Product UI:** let task frequency, information density, permissions, and state determine the shell.

## Good output

`Default reflex: rejected a centred campaign hero and three promo cards; selected Catalogue with an editorial product rail because the buyer needs category scanning and material comparison.`

## Bad output

`Default reflex: made it unique and modern.`

The gate must name a concrete rejected/kept choice and a concrete reason. Do not turn it into ceremonial prose.
