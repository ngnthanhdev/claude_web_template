# Local services

Use for plumbers, electricians, cleaners, repair providers, trades, and appointment-based businesses where locality, availability, and operational trust drive conversion.

## Buyer questions to answer early

1. Do you serve my location?
2. Do you handle this specific problem?
3. When are you available, and is emergency service real?
4. What happens after I contact you?
5. Who arrives, what credentials apply, and how is pricing handled?

## Recommended sequence

Service + location proposition → contact/availability action → service coverage → problem/service categories → process and response expectations → credentials/policies → verified reviews or completed work → service area/contact details → FAQ.

The sequence may be compressed, but locality and contact path must not be delayed behind a generic company story.

## Contact and quote rules

- Use tap-to-call only with a real phone number and operating context.
- Label emergency service only when supplied by the business; include hours or limitations.
- Ask for postcode/address only when required to check coverage or dispatch.
- Keep the first form short: contact, location, problem category, optional message/photo.
- Confirmation must state response channel and expected timing using supplied facts or `to be confirmed`.
- Provide failure, retry, offline, and invalid-location states.

## Proof model

Prefer licences, insurance, certifications, named technicians, service guarantees, before/after work, review-source links, and clear policies. Do not invent any of them. When proof is unavailable, use transparent placeholders or restructure around process and scope.

## Structural guidance

- Split Studio fits service + proof pairings.
- Narrative Workflow fits diagnose → quote → schedule → complete.
- Conversational FAQ fits high-anxiety or unfamiliar services.
- C4 Sticky Bottom Bar may carry call/book on mobile, but never obscure content or duplicate an emergency banner.
- N12 Announcement Banner is appropriate only for a real service notice, seasonal offer, or availability update.

## Distinction from professional services

Local-service trust is operational: area, arrival, safety, availability, and practical completion. Professional-service trust is epistemic: expertise, method, judgement, credentials, and outcomes. Do not give a plumber a strategy-consulting narrative or give an advisory firm an emergency-dispatch page.

## Anti-patterns

- Fake `24/7`, `same-day`, years-in-business, ratings, locations, or licence badges.
- City-name stuffing or doorway-page copy.
- Stock technician photos presented as the real crew.
- A long quote form before service coverage is known.
- Equal-weight `Call`, `Book`, `Email`, `Chat`, and `Get quote` buttons competing in the hero.
- Decorative maps without address, service radius, directions, or accessible text equivalent.
