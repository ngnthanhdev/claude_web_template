# Professional services

Use for consultants, advisory firms, accountants, legal services, specialist agencies, and other expertise-led B2B engagements.

## Buyer questions to answer

1. Is this firm specialised in my situation?
2. What concrete business problem and outcome do they address?
3. What is their method, and what will working together involve?
4. What credible evidence supports the claim?
5. What commitment is required for the first conversation?

## Recommended sequence

Audience + problem/outcome proposition → service or engagement options → method/framework → relevant case studies → named expertise/credentials → commercial model when supplied → objections/FAQ → discovery or proposal CTA.

Do not force pricing tiers when work is bespoke. A clear engagement model, starting condition, or `request a scoped proposal` is more honest than invented packages.

## Proof model

Rank proof in this order:

1. Relevant case study with context, intervention, and attributable outcome.
2. Named expert with real role, experience, and credentials.
3. Verifiable client quote or logo with permission/source.
4. Published method, research, framework, or demonstration.
5. Transparent process and boundaries when stronger proof is unavailable.

Unsupported years, percentages, client counts, awards, ROI, and satisfaction scores are prohibited.

## Funnel choices

- **Lead generation:** use a short qualification form when routing depends on company, problem, or engagement scope.
- **Click-through:** route to service detail, booking, or a proposal brief when more context is needed first.
- **Subscription:** offer a real report, briefing, or newsletter with explicit frequency and value.
- **Video-led:** use a case walkthrough, method explanation, webinar excerpt, or expert introduction as evidence.

Load [`../conversion-funnels.md`](../conversion-funnels.md) and select one primary mode rather than treating these as four separate site categories.

## Structural guidance

- Narrative Workflow fits a defensible consulting method.
- Quote-Led fits a strong, attributable client result.
- Split Studio fits service/expertise and proof pairings.
- Long Document fits a founder-led thesis or advisory letter.
- Stat-Led is allowed only when the supplied metric is credible, contextualised, and sourced.
- C2 Inline Form should request no more than the fields needed for initial qualification.

## Distinction from local services

Professional-service buyers evaluate judgement, fit, methodology, authority, and commercial risk. Local-service buyers evaluate service area, availability, arrival, safety, and task completion. The same `Get a quote` button does not make the architectures interchangeable.

## Anti-patterns

- Vague claims such as `unlock potential`, `transform your business`, or `drive growth` without audience, mechanism, or outcome.
- Fake client logos, case-study ROI, testimonials, awards, experts, or satisfaction statistics.
- Stock portraits presented as the real consulting team.
- Repeated placeholder descriptions across different services.
- Decorative laptop/3D imagery presented as capability evidence.
- Pricing tables for bespoke engagements without a real commercial basis.
- Long forms that demand budget, phone, revenue, and timeline before providing enough context or trust.
