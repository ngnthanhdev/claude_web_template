# Style directions

Use this as a direction index, not a template catalogue. Select one primary direction and optionally one supporting influence. Preserve the domain's content, proof, and interaction contracts underneath the art direction.

## Direction index

| Direction | Reach for it when | Signature moves | Reject when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet luxury | provenance, craft, high price, restraint | large material photography, low-chroma palette, precise serif/sans pairing, sparse actions | the catalogue is price-led or highly promotional |
| Editorial retail | curation and cultural context sell the product | issue-like pacing, captions, irregular product stories, index navigation | fast comparison and dense filtering are primary |
| Technical industrial | specifications and compatibility drive purchase | tabular facts, diagrams, exposed grid, utilitarian controls, restrained signal colour | the product is bought mainly through emotion/taste |
| Tactile organic | food, wellness, craft, home, natural materials | scanned texture, imperfect edges, macro crops, warm tinted neutrals | texture harms legibility or implies false provenance |
| Vivid pop | playful consumer brand, youth culture, gifting | disciplined high-chroma blocks, bold type, sticker-like accents, characterful transitions | regulated, high-anxiety, or data-dense decisions |
| Neo-brutal commerce | price, drops, culture, urgency with a confident brand | hard rules, slab controls, visible inventory, abrupt scale shifts | accessibility or brand trust depends on calm reassurance |
| Spatial product | form, finish, configuration, or physical interaction is proof | manipulable object, exploded view, material/colour switching, spatial annotation | the 3D is decorative or a still would communicate equally well |
| Kinetic type | language and campaign message are the product | variable-type transitions, controlled marquee, type-led reveal | long-form reading, localisation, or reduced-motion would collapse the concept |
| Dense marketplace | breadth and discovery are the value | search-first shell, category matrix, compact product density, saved/filter state | the shop has only a small focused range |
| Maximal collage | culture, fashion, music, events, experimental brands | layered crops, mixed scale, cut-paper composition, deliberate colour collisions | product facts or purchase controls become hard to find |

## Colour discipline

“Colourful” does not mean using every hue at equal strength.

- Choose one dominant field, one functional accent, and up to two supporting hues.
- Give state colours reserved meaning; do not reuse error red as decoration.
- Keep text/surface contrast valid across every block.
- Use full-spectrum/rainbow treatment only when spectrum itself carries meaning.
- Keep all colours tokenised after the direction is locked.

## Trend freshness protocol

When the user explicitly asks for the latest/current trend:

1. Inspect recent, dated work from multiple reputable design showcases, studios, or award programmes when browsing is available.
2. Extract recurring structural, type, colour, image, and motion behaviours; do not copy a showcased site.
3. Record the scan date and source links in the working notes or diagnosis.
4. Translate the observed behaviour into the selected domain's needs.
5. Prefer a durable direction over a named micro-trend that has no functional fit.

Never claim a direction is “the latest global trend” without a dated scan. Without browsing, call it a style direction, not a current trend.

## Combination rules

- Combine at most two directions.
- One direction owns layout/type; the supporting influence may affect texture, colour, or motion only.
- Never combine multiple trends merely to appear original.
- Run the default-reflex gate after combining: a fashionable combination can still be an AI default.
